The open question

The genuine Sony job-chain module is staged, loaded to 0xA00, and entered — with no halts, faults, or access violations anywhere in the run. It then returns immediately and does no work. The kernel contract it depends on is verified identical to LLE, and its workload argument is correct (arg=0x1eca280, matching the third job chain the title created), so the reason it bails is still unknown.

  1. Capture local store while wid=6 is current — the existing capture caught a SYS_SRV moment and proves nothing about the copy.
  2. Trace its first few hundred instructions under HLE against LLE and find the branch that leaves early.
  3. Verify jobChain->pc and the descriptor list are populated — the job chain address reaching the SPU is confirmed correct, so if pc is null the defect is on the PPU side — RunJobChain and KickJobChain log at trace level, so "never called" is not evidence.
